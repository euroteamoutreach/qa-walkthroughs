Not browser-testable
  • Suppressed notification / empty state. Deactivating a distributor with no onboarded users sends no email, and the Activation tab shows "No users will be notified." Every seeded distributor has an onboarded primary contact, so staging this in the browser means clearing onboarding first — it's covered by Admin::DistributorsControllerTest ("destroy sends nothing when there are no onboarded users") and a system test for the empty-state disclosure.
  • Demoted-admin email fallback. If the assigned admin has been demoted to a non-admin, the deactivation email drops the "contact your administrator" line and falls back to a "reply to this email" support line — matching the landing page's routable_admin fallback. Awkward to stage in-browser; covered by DistributorMailerTest.
  • Reactivation email content. Verified via the mailer preview at and by DistributorMailerTest; in the browser you only observe that reactivation restores access.
