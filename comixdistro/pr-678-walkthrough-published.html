Verifying on production? Use the production app and your own account in place of the local server and seed logins below. The seed accounts exist only locally — on production, use a real admin account, a real distributor account, and sign up a throwaway address you control for the mid-onboarding part. The steps and expected results are identical.

The bug (a regression from #669): a distributor stuck in the first two onboarding steps — before their Distributor record exists — had no "Get help" entry in the menu and was bounced if they reached the form. This PR makes the in-app support form reachable for any non-admin throughout onboarding, tolerating a missing distributor end to end.

Walkthrough
Part 1 — Mid-onboarding distributor reaches support new in this PR

The core fix. A brand-new signup has no distributor until they submit the Organization step, so steps 1–2 are the distributor-less window.

  1. Go to , register with a fresh address (e.g. ), and click the magic link in the letter_opener tab.
    ✅ You land in onboarding at Step 1 of 6 (Profile) — signed in, but with no distributor yet.
  2. Open the user menu (avatar, top-right).
    ✅ A "Get help" entry appears alongside Profile / language / Sign out. (Before this PR it was absent here — the bug.)
  3. Click Get help.
    ✅ The support form opens with no error. The "We'll include these details" card shows your Name and Email only — the distributor number / organization / location rows are omitted.
  4. Choose a Category (e.g. "Other question"), type a Message, and click Send request.
    ✅ You're returned to your onboarding step with a green "Thanks for reaching out…" confirmation — not a red "Please complete your profile" error. (This is the folded-in fix; the success notice used to get swallowed.)
  5. (Optional, team side.) Check the letter_opener tab(s).
    ✅ The team notification is addressed to all admins, its subject reads "…onboarding in progress" (no distributor number), and the body notes a user still completing onboarding submitted it — no distributor section or admin deep-link. You also receive an acknowledgement email.
Part 2 — Completed distributor still works pre-existing — for context
  1. Sign in as (magic link) → you land on the home dashboard.
  2. Open the user menu → click Get help.
    ✅ The info card now includes the distributor rows (number, organization, location) on top of name/email.
  3. Submit a request.
    ✅ You land on the home page with the green confirmation. Routing is unchanged — the request goes to this distributor's assigned admin and records an activity entry (visible to admins under Activity).
Part 3 — Admin is sent home pre-existing boundary
  1. Sign in as → admin dashboard.
  2. Open the admin user menu.
    ✅ There is no "Get help" entry (admins have direct contact channels).
  3. Visit directly.
    ✅ You're redirected to the admin dashboard.
