Walkthrough
Part 1 — Distributor files a support request new in this PR
  1. Signed in as a distributor, open the user menu (avatar, top-right).
    ✅ The dropdown shows «Потрібна допомога» (Get help) between Профіль and the language switcher.
  2. Click «Потрібна допомога».
    ✅ Lands on the support form. An info card reads «Ми додамо ці дані до вашого запиту» and lists your name, email, phone, distributor number, organization, and location — all prefilled.
  3. Click «Надіслати запит» (Send) with both fields empty.
    ✅ Form re-renders with a red error summary «Категорія не може бути пустим» / «Повідомлення не може бути пустим», red field outlines, and inline «не може бути пустим». Labels and errors are fully Ukrainian.
  4. Pick a category (e.g. «Щось не працює»), type a message, and submit.
    ✅ Redirects to Home with a green flash: «Дякуємо за звернення — ми отримали ваше повідомлення й невдовзі відповімо.»
  5. Email check. Local: the letter_opener tab(s) that pop on send. Production: the team support inbox + your own inbox.
    ✅ Two emails — a team notification (To the assigned admin, Cc the support inbox, Reply-To your email, in Ukrainian, with your details + message) and an acknowledgement back to you, in your own locale.
  6. Locale check: switch the app language to English, reopen the form.
    ✅ Labels read Category / Message and the category options are in English.
  7. Mobile check: view the form at ~375px (device toolbar → iPhone SE, or a real phone).
    ✅ Single-column layout: info card stacks to one column, the select and textarea are full-width, nothing overflows horizontally. (Native category picker is the OS picker — proper size on a real device.)
Part 2 — Public "Contact" link new in this PR
  1. In the page footer (visible logged-out on the sign-in page and logged-in), find the link next to «Умови участі».
    ✅ A «Контакти» (Contact) link is present; it opens the marketing-site contact form in a new tab.
  2. Mobile check: view the footer at ~375px.
    ✅ The three items stack into centered lines with no separator dots (no orphaned dot). Widen past ~640px and they return to a single inline row separated by · dots.
Part 3 — Admin sees the request on the dashboard new in this PR
  1. Signed in as an admin, land on the dashboard.
    ✅ Under «Остання активність» (Recent activity), the top entry reads «<distributor> звернувся по допомогу (<category>) — надіслано: <admin>» with an amber info icon.
  2. Click the entry's timestamp link.
    ✅ Navigates to that distributor's admin page.
  3. Open the full activity feed (/admin/activity) and select the Distributors tab.
    ✅ The support-request entry appears there (it's tracked on the distributor — by design).
