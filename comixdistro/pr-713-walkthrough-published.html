Verifying on production? Use the production app and your own distributor account (and an email address you control for the invitee) in place of the local server and seed logins below. The steps and expected results are identical.

What changed: If an invitee opens their signup form and the invite is then resent (which rotates the token), expires, or is revoked before they submit, the old form now carries a token that no longer resolves. Previously the app silently created the person a brand-new account in their own duplicate organization (a 6-step onboarding). Now it rejects the submission with a clear “Invitation not found” page — no account, no duplicate org. The original invite (under its new token) stays valid.

Walkthrough
Part 1 — Resent invite link is rejected, not turned into a duplicate org new in this PR
  1. Sign in as and go to the Team page:
    ✅ “Team” page with an “Invite a team member” form.
  2. In the invite form, enter a new email — locally (on production, an address you control) — and submit.
    ✅ Flash “Invitation sent to …”; the email appears under Pending invites. Locally, the invite email opens in a new tab.
  3. ⚠ Open the invite link in a separate incognito/private window (or a browser where you are signed out). Copy the “Join the team” link from the invite email and open it there. Do not submit the form yet.
    ✅ “Join Київська книжкова місія” signup form, with the invited email pre-filled.
    ⚠️ Gotcha: if you open the link while signed in as the distributor, the app redirects you to the dashboard — invite acceptance is a signed-out flow. Use incognito.
  4. Switch back to the distributor window. On the Team page, next to the pending invite, click Resend.
    ✅ Flash “Invitation resent to …”. (Behind the scenes this rotates the invite’s token, so the link still open in the incognito window is now stale.)
  5. Return to the incognito signup form (still open from step 3). Fill in First name and Last name, then click Sign up.
    ✅ NEW: the page shows “Invitation not found — This invitation link is not valid. Please check the link in your email and try again.” No account is created and no new organization appears.
    ❌ Before this fix: the invitee would have been signed up into a brand-new organization of their own, with a 6-step (instead of 5-step) onboarding.
  6. Confirm the happy path still works. In the incognito window, open the new invite link from the resent email (the most recent one), fill in First/Last name, and click Sign up.
    ✅ Redirects to “Sign in with magic link” (“check your email”). Completing the magic-link sign-in lands the user in onboarding joining Київська книжкова місія (5 steps — no “create organization” step), not a new org.
Part 2 — Same guard covers a cancelled (revoked) invite new in this PR — optional

The fix treats resent, expired, and revoked tokens identically. To spot-check the revoked case:

  1. Send a fresh invite (Part 1, steps 1–3) and open its link in incognito; don’t submit.
  2. In the distributor window, click Cancel next to that pending invite and confirm.
    ✅ The invite disappears from Pending invites.
  3. Back in the incognito form, fill the name fields and click Sign up.
    ✅ Same “Invitation not found” page; no account created.
Not browser-testable
  • Query de-duplication (memoized invite lookup): an internal performance tweak so the stale-check and the association share one query — no visible behavior; covered by the integration tests.
  • Tamper guard preserved: submitting a valid token with a mismatched email still creates a normal solo account (not the stale path). This is hard to trigger through the UI (the email field is locked to the invite) and is covered by the existing integration test.
