  1. Log in as admin and go to Admin → Events filtered by approved:

  4. Scroll to the bottom of the Report tab panel.
    ✅ Two distinct action sections appear, separated by horizontal lines:

  5. [Mobile check] Resize the browser to 375px wide.
    ✅ Both the approve button and the reject form stack and remain fully visible and tappable — no overflow or clipping.
  6. Click "Схвалити звіт і завершити подію".
    ✅ A confirmation dialog appears: "Схвалити цей звіт і позначити подію як завершену?" with a sky-blue confirm button also labeled "Схвалити звіт і завершити подію".
  7. Click the confirm button.
    ✅ The page reloads showing:
      • Status badge: "Завершено"
      • Green success flash: "Подію позначено як завершену."
